# Research Decision

The research phase selected **P37 — partial-refund allocation and clawback on split payments**.

The selection is supported by strong Razorpay-specific evidence, narrow buildable scope, meaningful payments-engineering failure modes, measurable allocation correctness, and a defensible AI boundary.

The intended intelligent scope remains deliberately small: interpret a merchant/platform agreement into a structured rule, require human confirmation, then run deterministic allocation using the confirmed rule. Broad autonomous money movement and multi-agent orchestration are not the starting point.

The benchmark exists first so future approaches are compared against independent truth rather than against a predictor-derived label.
